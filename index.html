Machine Learning

ES 335 · IIT Gandhinagar · Semester II, 2023–24

ES 335 is an introduction to machine learning: decision trees and ensembles, linear and logistic regression, gradient descent, neural networks and CNNs, nearest neighbours, unsupervised and reinforcement learning, and support vector machines. Most lectures link their slides, a full class recording and notebooks.

Questions

Is there a project?

Not a separate one. Each assignment has a project-like question instead, the same for all students, such as building an activity classifier from public data and then from data you collect on your smartphone.

What are the prerequisites?

There are no formal prerequisites, but you are expected to know Python programming and data structures (ES 242 or equivalent), probability and statistics, linear algebra and calculus. You must clear the prerequisite test to take the course.

What if I miss a quiz?

A missed quiz is marked zero and there are no makeups, for any reason. Only the best three of four quizzes count, which is meant for exactly this case.

Is there a mid-semester or end-semester exam?

Two of the four quizzes are held in the mid-semester and end-semester exam slots; the other two are held during the semester. All are closed book.

Can I use my laptop in class?

No. Laptops are not allowed in class for any reason.
